# Linking Prognostic Fire Emissions to Atmospheric Chemistry and Aerosols in GFDL Atmospheric and Land Model version 4.2 (AM4.2/LM4.2)

This preprint links prognostic fire emissions to atmospheric chemistry and aerosols in GFDL's coupled AM4.2/LM4.2 system, extending fire treatment beyond prescribed emissions.

The study examines how prognostic fire emissions affect atmospheric composition and aerosol evolution in the coupled model framework.
